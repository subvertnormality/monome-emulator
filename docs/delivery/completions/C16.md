## Measured speed

`artifacts/c16/m5-speed-06d.json` compares each real-time session's wall duration
with the median of its three controlled sessions, including input setup and
cleanup. Autosave is 3.55 times faster (84.11s versus 23.69s). MIDI transport,
handoff and modulation cases improve by approximately 1.05–1.25 times.
Editing and long phrase cases are slower: ratios range from 0.53 to 0.89 times.
Controlled time currently provides exact reproducible timing, not a universal
speedup. Investigate polling/capture throughput during the remaining campaign;
do not weaken event or timing assertions to improve these numbers.
